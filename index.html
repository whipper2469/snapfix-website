About the studio

Claybird Labs is an independent software studio based in Kitchener–Waterloo, Ontario. We design and build our own apps end to end — the idea, the interface, the backend, and the support inbox.

We like problems that are annoying, specific, and solvable: the kind where a good app saves someone a phone call, a service fee, or an afternoon. We'd rather ship one app that people keep than five they delete.

Everything here is built and maintained in-house. If you email us, you get the person who wrote the code.

At a glance

  • Based in Kitchener–Waterloo, Ontario, Canada
  • Independent and self-funded
  • Native iOS apps, built with Swift and SwiftUI
  • Every app has a real support address, answered by a human
