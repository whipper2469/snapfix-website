FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of problems can SnapFix diagnose?

Plumbing issues (leaks, drips, clogs), wall and ceiling damage, appliance error codes, electrical concerns like outlets and switches, pest identification, paint and surface damage, fixtures, and much more. If you can photograph it, SnapFix can help.

How accurate is the AI diagnosis?

SnapFix shows a confidence score with every diagnosis so you always know how certain the AI is. You can add up to three photos from different angles for a more accurate result, and use the follow-up chat to ask questions about your specific situation.

Is SnapFix a replacement for a professional?

SnapFix gives you a clear understanding of the problem and step-by-step guidance for repairs you can safely do yourself. For complex, dangerous, or code-regulated work — like major electrical or gas — we always recommend a licensed professional, and the app will tell you when that's the case.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photos are used to generate your diagnosis and are stored securely with your account so you can revisit past repairs. We never sell your photos or data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Which devices does SnapFix support?

SnapFix is available now for iPhone on the App Store (iOS 17 or later). An Android version is in the works.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → SnapFix → Cancel. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
